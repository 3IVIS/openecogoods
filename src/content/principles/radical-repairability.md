order: 1
title: Radical repairability
summary: Modular construction, standard fasteners, documented disassembly, and sourceable spare parts.
---

A design earns this if a competent owner — not just the original maker — can
take it apart, diagnose a failure, and fix it with common tools.

- Use standard, non-proprietary fasteners (no glued joints where a screw would do)
- Publish an exploded diagram and disassembly sequence with every design
- Design wear parts as separate, swappable components
- Avoid single-use adhesives or welds where a mechanical joint is feasible
