---
order: 5
title: Full lifecycle transparency
summary: Every design ships with a bill of materials, a rough impact estimate, and an end-of-life plan.
---

You can't improve what isn't measured, and a community can't make good
tradeoffs without seeing the numbers.

- Every published design includes a complete bill of materials
- Include a rough carbon/impact estimate, even if approximate — and show the method used
- State explicitly what happens to the product at end of life: recycle, compost, return, or reuse
- Update the estimate as the design changes, so it stays trustworthy
