---
order: 2
title: Material honesty
summary: Prefer mono-materials, avoid unrecyclable composites, favor recycled or rapidly-renewable inputs.
---

Mixed-material composites (plastic bonded to metal, foam laminated to fabric)
are cheap to manufacture and nearly impossible to recycle. We default away
from them.

- Prefer a single recyclable material per part wherever structurally possible
- When composites are unavoidable, design the joint to be separable at end of life
- Favor recycled feedstock and rapidly-renewable materials (bamboo, cork, reclaimed wood) over virgin extraction
- Every material choice in a design should be justified in its bill of materials
