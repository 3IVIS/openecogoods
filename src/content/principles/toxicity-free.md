---
order: 7
title: Toxicity-free
summary: Nothing on REACH SVHC or EPA watch lists in the finished product.
---

A product that's recyclable but poisons the people who make, use, or recycle
it isn't ecological.

- Exclude substances on the EU REACH Substances of Very High Concern list
- Exclude substances on relevant EPA watch lists
- Prefer finishes and coatings with published, verifiable safety data
- Flag any exception explicitly in the design docs, with the reasoning, rather than omitting it
