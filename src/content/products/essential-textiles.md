Textile production and disposal (especially fast fashion) is a top-tier
contributor to water use, microplastic pollution, and landfill volume. The
impact case is very strong. The feasibility case is weaker: garment
construction, sizing, and fabric sourcing are harder to standardize and
distribute than rigid-object designs.

**Why it scores this way:** highest-impact category on the list, but lowest
feasibility for a young open-hardware-style community to tackle first.

**By the numbers:** roughly 100 billion garments are produced globally each
year. The average piece of clothing is worn only 7–10 times before being
discarded, and about 65% of garments are thrown out within a year of
purchase — a usage rate that has reportedly dropped by roughly a third over
the last 15 years as "fast fashion" cycles accelerated.
