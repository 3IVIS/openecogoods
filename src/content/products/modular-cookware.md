order: 2
title: Modular, repairable cookware
impact: 4
feasibility: 3
summary: Non-stick coatings and bonded multi-layer pans are replaced constantly and can't be recycled or refinished.
---

Most cookware today bonds dissimilar metals and coatings together, so it's
thrown out the moment the coating fails. A mono-material, refinishable
design (cast iron, plain stainless) with swappable handles could last a
lifetime.

**Why it scores well:** high replacement frequency today, moderate
feasibility (metalworking is more specialized than woodworking, but well
within reach of existing maker/blacksmith communities).
