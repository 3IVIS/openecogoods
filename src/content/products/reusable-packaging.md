Packaging waste is enormous by volume and mostly single-use by design. An
open, standardized reusable container system (stackable, mono-material,
return-friendly) could displace a meaningful share of it — but it depends on
adoption and return logistics as much as the object itself.

**Why it scores well on impact, moderate on feasibility:** massive waste
volume, but success depends on network effects (return/reuse infrastructure)
that a single open design can't solve alone.

**By the numbers:** global plastic packaging production is estimated at
roughly 300–350 million tonnes a year, and packaging is projected to remain
the single largest share of plastic waste generation worldwide. Only about
9% of plastic packaging is recycled — the rest is landfilled, incinerated,
or leaks into the environment, almost always after a single use measured in
days or less.
